## Method / provenance

1. Candidate photo pages were discovered with web search restricted to `site:flickr.com`
   (targets: HR Vista, BeingHR, HR conclave/summit India, corporate conference India, BKC, Jio Grounds,
   Lavasa, Christ University Lavasa, cultural night, trekking/Sahyadri) and with Flickr's keyless
   public tag feeds (`api.flickr.com/services/feeds/photos_public.gne` for `lavasa`, `sahyadri`,
   `hrconclave`, `paneldiscussion`, ...).
2. Metadata (title, photographer, licence, aspect) came from Flickr's keyless oEmbed endpoint
   (`/services/oembed/?url=...`), and images were pulled at the largest size that exists
   (`_k`/`_h` where available, otherwise `_b` = 1024px on the long edge).
3. The `event-hrvista` rows reuse originals already harvested from the official CHRIST Lavasa
   photostream (1600x900) - see `docs/MEDIA_MANIFEST.json` for that earlier harvest's own listing.

## Known limitations (stated plainly)

- Most newly fetched Flickr photos top out at **1024px on the long edge** because that is the largest
  size Flickr exposes for them (`_h`/`_k` return 410 for those uploads). The 1600x900 `event-hrvista`
  images are the only >=1200px material, which is why every wide/full-bleed slot uses one.
  `hero-3.jpg` / `story-3.jpg` are 680x1024 portraits - that is the maximum size Flickr serves for them.
- `hero-2.jpg` is a genuine 1024x1024 square photo from an HR Conclave set; its Flickr page carries no
  description, so the content flag rests on the album/title context.
- Licensing on this page is reported exactly as Flickr's oEmbed endpoint returns it - no licence has been
  inferred or assumed.
